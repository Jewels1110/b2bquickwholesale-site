Support

Talk to the person who built it.

No ticket queue, no chatbot, no "level one". Email us and you'll get an answer from someone who can change the code.

Email

b2bquickwholesale@gmail.com

We aim to reply the same working day, and always within one business day.

What to include

Send these and we can usually answer in the first reply:

  • Your store address, for example yourstore.myshopify.com
  • A screenshot of the app's Setup checklist
  • What you expected, and what happened instead
  • If it's about a buyer: the company name and the buyer's email
  • If it's about a price: the product, and the price you expected

The assistant

There's an assistant in the bottom corner of this site, and another inside the app under Ask about your setup. The one in the app can see your store's checklist, so it can tell you what's wrong with your setup rather than in general. Either will hand you to a human the moment it isn't sure, and your conversation goes with the email.

It never receives your customers' names, emails or orders. How that works.

Asking for a feature

Tell us the situation, not the feature. "Half my buyers order the same twelve products every month" is more useful than "add favourites", and usually gets a better answer.

Security or privacy

Email the same address with "Security" in the subject and we'll respond as a priority. See the privacy policy for what data the app touches.

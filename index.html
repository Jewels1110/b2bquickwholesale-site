Quick answers

Do I need Shopify Plus?

No. B2B is included on Basic, Grow, Advanced and Plus. Plus adds catalogs assigned directly to individual companies; other plans assign them through B2B markets, and the ordering page works either way. Requirements.

Does this change my retail storefront?

No. Only signed-in wholesale buyers can use the ordering page, and retail shoppers never see wholesale shipping rates.

Where do the prices come from?

From the catalog you assign in Shopify. The app stores no prices of its own, so they can't drift out of sync.

What if I uninstall?

Remove the wholesale rates first, with the button in the app. Otherwise they stay behind and retail shoppers would see them.
